Discussion Questions

Question 1: Identifying Business Opportunities

Discuss how an entrepreneur can identify a business opportunity in Solomon Islands. Identify one problem in your community that could be turned into a business opportunity and explain why people would be willing to pay for the proposed solution.

Question 2: Problem-Solving Strategies

Choose one of these strategies and explain how it could help an entrepreneur solve a business problem. Use a practical example to demonstrate how the strategy could be applied.

Question 3: Testing a Business Idea

Before investing significant money in a new business, entrepreneurs need to determine whether their proposed solution meets customers' needs.
Imagine that your group wants to establish a small business providing construction materials in your community. Explain how you would test the business idea, collect feedback from potential customers, and determine whether the business is financially viable.
